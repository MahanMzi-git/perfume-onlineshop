# Aroma product terms

## Brand

The maker or label under which a product is sold. Several products can share one brand. A draft product can remain without a brand while the seller completes it.

## Product draft

A seller's unpublished product record. It is visible in the seller panel and absent from the public product detail API.

## Variant

A purchasable version of a product, identified by its SKU and attributes such as volume and concentration. A product may have several variants.
